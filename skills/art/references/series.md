# A coherent series

Recover the accepted series name, numbering convention, visual references, and
intended encounter. When these are consequential and unsettled, establish them
before producing a sequence. Treat proposed styles as provisional until accepted;
accepting a name does not settle the artwork. Do not invent the maker's reasons.

Distinguish recurring identity from expressive variation: masthead, number treatment,
type, placement, and framing can remain consistent while artwork responds to each
work's character. Use the actual approved assets. Inspect each cover at thumbnail
size and alongside another member before claiming series coherence.

Carry accepted corrections into subsequent pieces. Do not restart preference
discovery for settled choices or turn one correction into a universal style ban.
For cross-media work, explain the relationship between artwork and the supplied
programme or text without claiming an audition or reading that did not occur.
Specialists retain musical, factual, and authorship judgments; their absence does
not block independent visual work that has sufficient supplied context.
