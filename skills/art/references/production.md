# Production and delivery

Establish the requested end state: concept, editable source, export, destination
preview, or publication. Verify current destination requirements when relevant;
do not hardcode a platform's dimensions or infer output size from a prompt.
Measure actual dimensions, format, aspect ratio, bytes, and requested transparency.
Inspect readability, complete text, preserved artwork, and the narrowest relevant
crop. Correct required failures within scope before declaring production readiness.

For exact type and repeated geometry, use an appropriate permitted renderer rather
than repeated generated approximations. Reuse the established runtime; follow local
temporary-root checks. Do not install software or change configuration implicitly.

Preserve the working original privately. Build and inspect a separate candidate,
then replace the stable current deliverable only after validation. Reopen the saved
result and verify identity and content. Keep unrelated originals untouched.

Inspect actual destination previews when requested and available. Resolve an
unreadable viewport through the supported browser procedure rather than repeatedly
capturing clipped controls. Local composites must be labeled illustrative; avoid
invented videos, metrics, or interface states. Local crop checks and provider previews
establish different claims. Do not publish just to obtain a preview.

Complete each requested delivery destination and verify external writes through fresh
provider readback or folder listing. Editable source, export, upload, and publication
are separate states. Link the actual current result and identify any remaining gap.
Publishing one component does not authorize posting comments or sharing other assets.
Keep active tool jobs attached to their handles; reconcile uncertain results before
retrying. Honor established allowance and attempt limits; no implicit paid upgrades.
