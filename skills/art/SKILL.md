# Art

Help the person give an intention perceptible form and reconsider it through making.
Art spans images, writing, sound, performance, interaction, and space; available
production tools do not cover every medium. An interpretation or exploratory sketch
can be a complete result. Local agreements and specialist domain rules remain binding.

Attend to observable particulars before interpretation. Connect composition to what
the person wants someone to feel, notice, or understand. Let making reveal unexpected
possibilities, preserve productive ambiguity, and leave room for the audience's own
meaning. These are artistic practices, not claims about audience response.

## Encounter and make

Recover purpose, audience, requested medium, accepted references, and finish line
from the task. Ask only for consequential missing input. Inspect actual images,
rendered pages, slides, or recordings when the judgment depends on them; filenames,
captions, transcripts, and remembered appearances do not replace that encounter.
Distinguish observed details, the person's stated intention, and your interpretation.
Do not pretend to have seen or heard inaccessible material.

Choose tools by purpose, factual density, editability, and who will maintain the work.
Read the active provider skill and verify callable capabilities. Use existing authorized
accounts and destinations; capability availability does not prove access or allowance.
Do not silently change medium or provider to evade a limitation. Explain a missing
capability and continue only independent work that still serves the request.

For critique, consequential composition exploration or revision, or historical
adaptation, read [critique](references/critique.md).
For accepted references, source-bound text, product edits, or exact typography, read
[fidelity](references/fidelity.md). For recurring covers or a visual collection, read
[series design](references/series.md). For production assets and delivery, read
[production](references/production.md). Load only the relevant references.

## Revise and finish

Apply relevant corrections to the actual next composition choice. Preserve accepted
wording, visual relationships, and reasons for settled choices. A clarification does
not automatically authorize replacement of an existing emphasis. Make the requested
change and inspect both that change and what must remain. If the work already serves
the purpose, say why no further revision is needed.

Complete requirements already authorized before offering next actions. A required
font match, size correction, export, or delivery step is unfinished work, not an
optional new selection. Retain one current deliverable at a stable descriptive path;
keep candidates and prior working versions privately until validated replacement.

Report technical fidelity, artistic judgment, and the person's acceptance separately.
Label imagined scenes and destination mockups; they do not prove actual installation,
scale, audience response, or hosted state. A successful tool call is not inspection.
Use [review cases](references/review-cases.md) when validating this shared practice;
cases are not permission to rerun creative trials.

This skill grants no automatic saving, retention, import, account connection, spending,
sharing, messaging, or publication. Follow exact task authorization without repeatedly
asking for already-authorized actions. Personal setup and capture remain separate.
