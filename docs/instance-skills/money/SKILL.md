# Business Money

Money 1.0.0 is a shared business foundation. Local adapters determine adoption,
owners, available tools, and permitted destinations. This Core candidate remains
outside active rosters and generated packages; reading it grants no authority.

Help the person judge what the business can sustain and make possible. Start from
the actual request, relevant obligation or approved purpose, permitted evidence,
and the human who decides. Local agreements and specialist rules remain binding.
Routine arithmetic stays direct; it needs no new objective, intake, or research.
General nonfinancial effort belongs to the existing work practice.

## Assess the financial question

Distinguish dated available resources, estimates, proposed allocations, authorized
commitments, unpaid obligations, and observed results. Preserve currency, periods,
source references, uncertainty, and original estimates beside revisions. Keep
time and capacity in their own units; do not invent their monetary value.

For transaction classification, opening balances, personally paid company costs,
repayments, or reconciliation, read [bookkeeping basis](references/bookkeeping-basis.md).
Recover what the record measures before proposing an entry. A cash tracker is
not automatically accounting books, and a recorded obligation is not a payment.

For a business opportunity, affordability tradeoff, or consequential financial
research, read [opportunity assessment](references/opportunity-assessment.md).
Use only the parts the decision needs. Known purpose can come from the current
request or an obligation; do not require an intent system or invent priorities.
An existing financial-model, diligence, or market-analysis specialist retains
its route; use this practice only for the stewardship question that remains.

For invoices, collections, settlements, payables, cash forecasts, budget variance,
or product-business records, read [operating finance](references/operating-finance.md).
Connect the relevant records to the decision; do not require a full monthly close
for a narrow question. Reconcile conflicting dated sources by scope and evidence,
and distinguish reported account milestones from directly verified account state.

Google Sheets and XLSX competence are core capabilities. Before creating, editing,
importing, refreshing, or auditing a workbook, read
[spreadsheet practice](references/spreadsheet-practice.md) and the available
provider skill. These methods need actual tool verification in each workspace;
shared instructions alone do not establish execution capability.

For repeated imports, identity review, reconciliation or consequential arithmetic,
read [utility practice](references/utility-practice.md). Its optional standard-library
Python checks accept supplied JSON and return results without writing records.
Keep provider mechanics and human classification decisions with their owners.

## Records, tools, and authority

Use an available provider skill when calculations or artifacts need it. Establish
the intended account, exact file, reporting basis, and permitted inputs. Tool
availability does not establish file access or write permission. If a capability
is missing, report the dependent limitation and continue useful supported work;
do not silently change accounts, providers, or destinations.

Recommendations grant no spending, transfer, customer commitment, account access,
or record-editing authority. Reuse exact authorization already supplied for the
task; do not ask again merely because the work changes owners. Reading, editing,
saving externally, sharing, and communicating remain distinct actions. Before
the first external content write, verify through current provider metadata or a
destination listing that the exact file is in the authorized folder or workspace.
Creation success alone does not establish its location. If the location is wrong
or unverified, withhold content; correct it only within existing file-lifecycle
authority and verify the destination before writing. Otherwise report the specific
blocker and continue independent preparation. After an authorized external edit,
use fresh readback to verify the affected content, file identity, and destination;
distinguish local from externally saved results. The provider skill retains the
file-operation mechanics.

Keep private business evidence in its authorized destination outside Git. Sharing
a method does not pool balances, evidence, approvals, or decision authority.
Do not transfer another workspace's records merely because they are accessible.
This v1 excludes personal investments, account setup, payment execution, automatic
retention, and synchronization. It creates no ledger, database, mandatory receipt,
provider dependency, or new professional authority. Consequential accounting,
tax, or legal conclusions require current authoritative support and appropriate
professional review; unsupported treatment stays unresolved.

Return the relevant verified record or analysis, supported judgment or calculation,
material assumptions and exceptions, actual completion state, and any decision
still owned by a human. A proposal is
not approval; approval is not expenditure; a saved report is not verified payment.
Use [review cases](references/review-cases.md) for bounded manual evaluation and
[workspace adaptation](references/workspace-adaptation.md) only when preparing an
adoption or transfer proposal. Neither reference authorizes rollout.
