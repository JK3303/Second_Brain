# Personal workspace exchange contract

Status: review draft; inclusion does not establish adoption or activation.

This portable draft complements the [personal workspace standard](personal-workspace-standard.md).
It supplies no new permission to inspect, copy, retain, share, or act on personal
material. Existing local agreements and applicable permissions remain authoritative.

## Ownership and direction

The shared foundation maintains reusable methods and tools. Each person controls
their own intentions, personal material, approvals, and workspace adoption.
A mentoring role does not confer ownership of another person's records or decisions.

Into a personal workspace, transfer only reviewed tools, standards, blank templates,
fictional examples, or specifically authorized artifacts. Exclude the foundation's
private records, credentials, and other people's personal approvals.

From a personal workspace, inspect or receive named material for an authorized task.
Preserve authorship and attribution. Inspection, copying, retention, and action are
separate permissions. Access does not authorize onward sharing with another person;
anonymization alone does not supply permission.

## Retention and applicable permission

Before introducing another person's material into a captured conversation, verify
that permission covers that retention. Prefer a reference or the smallest necessary
excerpt. "Conversational only" means no additional artifact or canonical-memory
admission; application or provider logs may still persist. Existing authorized
retention workflows remain applicable. Access alone does not authorize memory or
learning-ledger admission.

Reuse applicable permissions without repeated ceremony. Reassess when the recipient,
purpose, content, retention, or proposed action changes. Cloning, repository transfer,
or opening a folder does not supply personal approvals.

If permission is withdrawn or expires, stop future use that depends on it, identify
known copies, and perform only authorized cleanup. Do not promise erasure from Git
history, backups, or provider systems. State remaining copies or unknown retention.

## Adoption and exchange evidence

Shared standards do not automatically override local working agreements or confirmed
intentions. Review adoption explicitly and preserve local adaptations. Packages
remain independently usable, with no automatic synchronization.

When an exchange needs a record, use the existing task rather than a new tracker:

- Material: the named artifact or bounded excerpt.
- Direction and purpose: who receives it and for which task.
- Authority: applicable permission and its scope.
- Retention: where it may persist and any limits.
- Result: what actually crossed, changed, or remained pending.

Local preparation, adoption, publication, transfer, activation, and archival are
separate states. Report each only to the extent supported by evidence.

## Technical limits and incidents

Where supplied, instance checks bind history access to the intended workspace,
private state, identity, and approvals. These are application boundaries, not
isolation between unrestricted processes under one operating-system account.
A separate repository or credential configuration does not establish that isolation.
Including this document does not install or enable such checks or history capture.

If material crosses incorrectly, stop the affected operation, disclose the scope,
preserve minimal evidence, and obtain missing cleanup authority. Do not broaden
inspection or delete unrelated records while investigating.
