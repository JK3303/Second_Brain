# Rebranding Method Pack

Reusable, client-neutral method for running a bounded rebranding engagement with human artistic direction and AI support.

## Scope of this folder

This folder contains **method only**. It contains no client research, no client decisions, no client assets, and no client preferences. Anything specific to a named engagement belongs in that engagement's approved private surface — see [`authority-boundaries.md`](authority-boundaries.md#repository-boundary).

## When to use this method

Use it when all of the following hold:

- A client organization has asked for brand direction, not brand execution.
- A human artistic director owns creative judgment and will personally make a recommendation.
- A named decision-maker at the client holds sole authority to select, reject, combine, hold, or request revision.
- The engagement ends at a review-ready decision package, and implementation is a separate, separately authorized piece of work.

Do not use it as an implementation method. It stops at the decision.

## Core commitments

1. **Three materially distinct territories, not three finishes on one idea.** Distinctness is tested at the level of strategic premise and who or what sits at the center of the brand — not at the level of palette or typeface. See the distinctness test in [`workflow.md`](workflow.md#step-4--territory-generation).

2. **Equal depth across proposals.** A proposal that is developed further than its siblings pre-decides the outcome. Depth parity is a validation gate, not a preference.

3. **Claim separation.** Every statement is labeled as a direct observation, an interpretation, a creative hypothesis, a human decision, or an unresolved claim. These are never blended into a single confident voice.

4. **Preserved uncertainty.** A missing fact is recorded as missing and routed to whoever can supply it. It is never replaced with a plausible assumption.

5. **No premature approval language.** Nothing is described as approved, selected, or agreed until the authorized decision-maker has said so and it has been recorded in a decision receipt.

6. **Baseline before improvement.** The first engagement run under this method is a measurement baseline. No efficiency or quality gain may be claimed until a later comparable engagement supplies evidence.
