# Collaboration Loop

The primary operating loop for human-AI creative collaboration:

```
human objective
  -> AI questions and restates the brief
  -> AI generates materially different options
  -> human critiques the options
  -> AI revises and explains the changes
  -> human records the lesson
  -> AI proposes a reusable rule
  -> human accepts, rejects, or holds the rule
```

## Step details

### 1. Human objective
The human states what they want to explore, create, or solve.

### 2. AI questions and restates
The AI asks clarifying questions if needed, then restates the brief to confirm understanding before generating.

### 3. AI generates options
The AI produces materially different options. Each option represents a distinct creative direction, not a minor variation. The AI explains its reasoning for each.

### 4. Human critiques
The human provides specific, actionable feedback on the options. The AI records the critique.

### 5. AI revises
The AI revises based on the critique and explains what changed and why. The AI does not silently drop elements of the critique.

### 6. Human records the lesson
The human identifies what was learned from the cycle.

### 7. AI proposes a rule
The AI extracts a candidate reusable rule from the lesson.

### 8. Human decides
The human accepts, rejects, or holds the proposed rule:
- **Accept** -> `memory/accepted-lessons.md`
- **Reject** -> `memory/rejected-lessons.md`
- **Hold** -> `memory/open-questions.md`
