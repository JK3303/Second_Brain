**Type definitions**

- **primary** — the organization's own published material, or material supplied directly by the client
- **secondary** — third-party description of the organization
- **client-supplied** — provided privately by the client for this engagement
- **observational** — directly observed by the AD or the team

**Reliability definitions**

- **high** — directly observed, or stated by the organization about itself and internally consistent
- **medium** — third-party report, single source, or organization claim that is material and unverified
- **low** — inferred, aggregated, or of uncertain origin

## 2. Claim ledger

Each row is one factual claim used in the engagement.

| # | Claim | Source ID | Claim type | Confidence | Verified independently | Used in |
|---|-------|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------------------|---------|
| C1 | | | observation / interpretation / hypothesis / human decision / unresolved | high / medium / low | yes / no / n/a | |

**Claim type definitions**

| Type | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **observation** | Directly recorded from a registered source |
| **interpretation** | A reading of observations; could be read differently |
| **hypothesis** | A creative proposition; not a claim about current reality |
| **human decision** | A choice made by a named human, with authority |
| **unresolved** | Asserted by a source but not confirmed, or contradicted between sources |

> A self-reported claim by the organization about itself is an **observation of the claim**, not a verification of it. Record it as `unresolved` where the claim is material.
