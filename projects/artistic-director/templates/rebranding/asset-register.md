## 1. Provenance labels

| Label | Meaning | May appear in review package? | May be used beyond review? |
|-------|---------|------------------------------|---------------------------|
| `sourced` | Obtained from an identified external origin | Yes, if licence permits review use | Only per licence terms |
| `generated` | Produced by a generative system | Yes, marked as generated | Requires human review; provenance disclosed |
| `placeholder` | Stands in for an asset not yet produced or cleared | Yes, **visibly marked on the artefact** | No — must be replaced |
| `self-created` | Made by the AD or team for this engagement | Yes | Per engagement terms |
| `rights-cleared` | Clearance obtained from an authorized party, on record | Yes | Yes, within the recorded clearance scope |

> `rights-cleared` is only applied when a named authorized party has granted clearance and it is recorded in §3. "No issue identified" is **not** clearance. Rights clearance is outside this engagement's authority — record status, do not obtain it.
