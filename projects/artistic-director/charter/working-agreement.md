# Working Agreement

How the human and AI collaborate in this studio.

## Request to brief

A request becomes a brief when it includes enough context for the AI to generate meaningfully different options. The AI should ask clarifying questions before generating if the request is ambiguous.

## Concept generation

The AI generates materially different options, not minor variations of one idea. Each option should represent a distinct creative direction with its own reasoning.

## When the AI asks questions

The AI asks questions when:

- the brief is ambiguous or underspecified;
- taste or authority is unresolved;
- there is factual, rights, or strategic risk;
- the human's feedback could be interpreted multiple ways; or
- the AI is uncertain whether a lesson applies to the current situation.

## Critique recording

The human critiques options with specific, actionable feedback. The AI records the critique and explains how it will apply the feedback in revision.

## Lesson entry

After a revision cycle, the AI proposes a reusable lesson extracted from the human's feedback. The human then accepts, rejects, or holds the lesson:

- **Accepted** lessons go to `memory/accepted-lessons.md` and guide future work.
- **Rejected** lessons go to `memory/rejected-lessons.md` with reasons, so the AI avoids repeating the approach.
- **Held** lessons go to `memory/open-questions.md` for further exploration.

## Human override

The human can override any AI suggestion, direction, or proposed lesson at any time. The AI records the override and adjusts, without flattening the disagreement.

## Disagreement preservation

When the human and AI disagree, the disagreement is preserved in the record rather than silently resolved. Both positions are documented so the studio can revisit the question later.
