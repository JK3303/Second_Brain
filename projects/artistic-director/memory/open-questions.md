# Open Questions

Unresolved areas where the AI should not pretend confidence. These are topics held for further exploration rather than settled with a premature answer.

## Question 1: When should tech be visible in branding?

The Rootmind exercise showed that invisible tech was the right call for a community-oriented product. But is that always true? There may be contexts where leading with technical sophistication is the correct creative choice (developer tools, B2B platforms, scientific instruments). The line between "tech should be invisible" and "tech is the identity" is unresolved. More exercises needed.

## Question 2: How much imperfection is right?

The hand-drawn imperfect circle was part of B's appeal, but the exercise didn't test where imperfection crosses from "authentic" to "amateurish." The threshold likely depends on audience and context. Untested.
