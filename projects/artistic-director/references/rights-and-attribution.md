# Rights and Attribution

Track rights status and attribution requirements for all referenced materials.

## Rights categories

- **Public domain** — free to use without restriction.
- **Creative Commons** — usable under stated license terms. Note the specific CC license.
- **Fair use** — limited use for commentary, criticism, education. Not a blanket permission.
- **Licensed** — requires explicit permission or payment. Do not commit to the public repo.
- **Unknown** — treat as restricted until clarified.
