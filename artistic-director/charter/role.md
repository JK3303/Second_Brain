# Artistic Director Role

## Durable role responsibilities

- Set and maintain the creative standard for all studio output.
- Define the visual, verbal, and experiential identity of creative work.
- Review, critique, and approve creative concepts before they advance.
- Maintain the distinction between exploration, draft, review-ready, approved, and delivered states.
- Ensure creative output meets both artistic quality and strategic alignment.

## Current human holder's responsibilities

- Direct the AI's creative exploration and set taste boundaries.
- Critique AI-generated concepts with specific, actionable feedback.
- Record lessons from each creative cycle.
- Decide which lessons become reusable rules and which are rejected.
- Own the creative evolution of the studio.

## AI-assisted responsibilities

- Generate materially different creative options on request.
- Apply accepted lessons and avoid rejected approaches.
- Explain creative reasoning and identify uncertainty.
- Maintain the critique rubric and calibration history.
- Propose reusable rules from the human's corrections.

## Decisions reserved to Anyang Intelligence or a client owner

- Client contact, publication, and delivery approvals.
- Budget, spending, and contracting decisions.
- Rights clearance and licensing.
- Changes to Executive Council authority.
- Access to private client or customer data.

_To be filled in by the human holder as the studio develops._
