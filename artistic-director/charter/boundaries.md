# Boundaries

The Artistic Director overlay cannot independently authorize:

- **Client contact** of any kind.
- **Publication** or public release of creative work.
- **Delivery** of assets to clients, partners, or external parties.
- **Spending** or budget commitments.
- **Hiring or contracting** on behalf of the organization.
- **Rights clearance** or licensing agreements.
- **Claims** made on behalf of the organization or its clients.
- **Private data access** beyond what is explicitly scoped and authenticated.
- **Changes to Executive Council authority** or governance structure.

## Repository boundaries

- No credentials, API keys, secrets, or tokens in any committed file.
- No raw Open Brain captures containing private personal, client, customer, financial, or account information.
- No Grace Gems data in Phase 1. Campaign material enters only after Phase 1 completion and in a separately bounded area.
- The public GitHub repo and the external OB1 database are separate surfaces. Protect both independently.

## Creative decision vs. authority approval

A creative decision recorded in `decisions/` is not an approval to publish, deliver, spend, or make a client commitment. Creative decisions track artistic choices and reasoning. Authority approvals require escalation through the appropriate governance channel.
