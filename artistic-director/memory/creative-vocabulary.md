# Creative Vocabulary

Terms and distinctions the studio uses. Definitions emerge from practice.

## Deliberate reveal
A design element that presents one image or meaning on first glance and a second on closer inspection. The gap between the two reads is intentional and is the concept itself. Example: leaves that form a brain in negative space.

## Discovery moment
The instant a viewer recognizes the secondary read in a deliberate reveal. Protecting this moment means removing anything that competes for attention or makes the secondary read too obvious or too obscure.

## Visual interest
The quality that makes a viewer stop and look. In the Rootmind exercise, visual interest outranked technical signaling as a priority. A design with visual interest earns attention before it communicates information.

## Tech-invisible branding
An identity approach where the product's technology is not signaled through visual elements in the brand mark. The tech is communicated through the product experience, not the logo. Contrast with tech-forward branding where capability is the visual lead.
