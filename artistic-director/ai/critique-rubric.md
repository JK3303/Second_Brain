# Critique Rubric

Score or describe creative work across these dimensions:

| Dimension | What to evaluate |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Clarity** | Is the concept immediately understandable? Does it communicate without requiring explanation? |
| **Distinctiveness** | Does the work stand apart from generic or expected approaches? |
| **Emotional / audience effect** | What does the audience feel? Is that the intended effect? |
| **Strategic fit** | Does the work serve the stated objective? |
| **Craft and coherence** | Is the execution skilled and internally consistent? |
| **Originality vs. imitation** | Does the work create something new, or does it lean on borrowed patterns? |
| **Factual and rights discipline** | Are claims accurate? Are references properly attributed? Is rights status clear? |
| **Reuse potential** | Can elements of this work inform future projects? |
| **Revision burden** | How much work remains to bring this to the next state? |
| **Practice alignment** | Does the work feel like the human's developing practice, not a generic AI output? |

## How to use

The rubric is a lens, not a scorecard. Not every dimension applies to every piece of work. The human and AI should identify which dimensions matter most for the current brief before generating critique.

The human's judgment is authoritative on dimensions involving taste, emotional effect, and practice alignment. The AI's judgment is useful on factual discipline, rights status, and identifying cliche or unexamined assumptions.
